# Monetization plan — implementation pending

No payment, advertising, premium-entitlement or analytics service has been enabled. Current usage counters record page use, not successful executions, unique users, revenue or billable consumption. Request throttles are process-local and are not durable customer quotas.

Before monetization: verify existing operations; establish consent-aware execution events and retention; measure provider costs with bounded budgets; implement durable quotas and entitlement checks on the server; then evaluate ads or paid features using the market research. Pricing and revenue projections require evidence. Paid infrastructure, billing activation and deployment require authorization.
