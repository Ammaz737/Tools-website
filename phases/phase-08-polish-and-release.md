# Phase 08 - Polish And Release

This phase hardens the product after the major tool families are in place.

## Objective

Turn a feature-complete toolbox into a reliable production-ready product.

## Scope

- SEO completion
- accessibility audit
- performance tuning
- route smoke tests
- legal pages
- ads placeholders
- privacy review
- release readiness

## Recommended Batch Order

### Batch 08A - Quality Checks

- route smoke tests
- shared logic tests
- regression review on key tool families

### Batch 08B - Accessibility Pass

- keyboard audit
- focus visibility
- labels and announcements
- reduced-motion review
- mobile layout review

### Batch 08C - SEO Completion

- unique metadata validation
- JSON-LD coverage
- sitemap verification
- OG and Twitter tag review

### Batch 08D - Performance Pass

- bundle review
- lazy import verification
- heavy route profiling
- worker offload review
- font and image optimization

### Batch 08E - Product Readiness

- legal pages
- privacy review
- ad slots
- content cleanup
- final launch checklist

## UI Guardrails

- Final polish must strengthen the established design system, not redesign it
- Fix visual inconsistencies by aligning to shared rules rather than inventing exceptions
- Review accent discipline, border usage, spacing rhythm, and typography hierarchy across major pages
- Verify mobile and desktop views both preserve the editorial product identity
- Ensure ads, legal copy, and SEO additions do not dilute the product feel

## Exit Criteria

- Tool pages meet the definition of done from `CLAUDE.md`
- The app performs well on real devices
- The project is launch-ready with clear quality standards

## Risks To Avoid

- Leaving accessibility and performance until after launch
- Assuming route rendering means tools are production-ready
- Shipping incomplete metadata across a large tool surface
